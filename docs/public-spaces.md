# Organic public spaces

The organic generator reserves one civic site before placing buildings. A separate seeded random stream keeps the reservation deterministic without consuming the geography stream. The site counts toward the existing park target: district sites are 32 × 28 m at the 500 m reference size; city sites are 48 × 40 m. District site dimensions follow its existing object scale. Layout proportions and tree crowns adapt to the individual park, not the whole city's geographic scale; benches and lamps retain their existing fixed renderer dimensions.

The reservation uses the existing conservative water disk, road/sidewalk extent and bounded placement attempts. Both building and park placement respect the larger of the building spacing requirement and the park clearance against occupied rectangles. Remaining parks are placed on clear land after buildings, without deleting buildings or lowering requested generation targets. If no civic rectangle fits within the preset's park-attempt budget, reservation is omitted and ordinary green-park placement continues; no unsafe or undersized civic rectangle is forced into occupied land.

Requested building counts remain bounded targets, not universal minimums. Reserving civic land can modestly reduce realized counts under the existing attempt cap (observed district seed 123: 181 to 178; seed 2026: 184 to 180). The explicit 110-building/seven-park profile remains verified.

`createParkLayout` produces the existing `parkPaths` and amenity data. The civic site has two intersecting paths, a paved central plaza and fountain, an entrance, benches, lamps and corner trees. Green parks retain most of their grass, with a narrow path, entrance and seating. The existing urban-detail renderer and organic terrain draping consume this data; no new renderer, asset download or dependency is required. Benches and lamps have fixed renderer dimensions, so tests check rendered footprints in addition to metadata radii.

These are public-space geometry and potential destinations, not implemented citizen visits, daily activity, service capacity or accessibility routing. An entrance stops at the park boundary; it is not a navigable sidewalk connection. Paved areas do not change the hydraulic model: infiltration still treats the whole park uniformly. Actual default/storm appearance and pause/reset acceptance require browser inspection and are not proven by unit tests.
