- S4 — Accepted approach: "Distribuir barrios con distintas densidades: centro, viviendas, comercios y zonas verdes." Generate distinguishable neighborhood density/height envelopes and irregular urban boundaries; preserve coherent IDs/home/node/shelter references and dry access.
